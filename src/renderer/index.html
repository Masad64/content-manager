📺 How to use Munshi

A quick ~1-minute tour — niches, sheets, channels, research and more.

Quick guide

  1. Niches → create your niche (Horror, Mystery…).
  2. Open the niche → paste your Google Sheet link in Attach sheet ("Anyone with link – Viewer"). Content imports in.
  3. Press ⬇ Download on a video → it's saved into the tool's folder.
  4. Channels → sign in once with "Connect YouTube", then press ⬆ Upload on a video.
  5. Performance shows uploaded vs remaining, Team has your members.

Help

• App won't open / shows an error → close it and open again.
• Sheet won't import → the sheet must be shared "Anyone with link – Viewer".
• Browser asks to sign in / verify during upload → do it, then it continues.
• Access / login issue → contact your admin.
